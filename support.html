English

Contact

piankecard@gmail.com — 片刻 Pianke. Please include your device model and iOS version; if it is about a card that was read wrongly, a photo of the card helps enormously, and you are of course free to black out anything you do not want to share.

Before you write: the three things that come up most

  • A field came out wrong, or empty. Tap the field in the review screen, then tap the text on the photo of the card — the app fills that field from what you tapped. Recognition is done on your device and is not perfect, especially on glossy cards, unusual layouts, or a photo taken at an angle.
  • I bought Pro and it is not showing. Open the upgrade screen and tap Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so the same purchase works on your other devices signed in with the same account. If it still does not appear, write in — do not buy it again.
  • Where are my cards stored, and how do I move them to a new phone? On the device only, never on a server. Use Settings → Backup to write a backup file, put it somewhere you control (AirDrop, Files, a computer), and restore it on the new phone. An encrypted .piankevault backup needs the passphrase you chose — if you lose it, the backup cannot be opened, including by us, because we never had it.

Reporting a problem

The most useful report is the smallest one that reproduces: what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. Screenshots are welcome. Please do not send anything you would not want a stranger to read — email is not private, and we would rather have a vaguer report than your customer's contact details.

What support cannot do

There is no account system and no copy of your data anywhere but your device. That is the whole design of the app, and the cost of it is real: a card deleted without a backup is gone, and a forgotten vault passphrase cannot be reset. Neither is a limitation we can lift for you on request.

Privacy

The iOS privacy policy is at privacy-policy-ios.html. Short version: the app has no networking code at all and collects nothing.

繁體中文

聯絡方式

piankecard@gmail.com(片刻 Pianke)。來信請附上裝置型號與 iOS 版本;如果是某張名片辨識錯誤, 附上那張名片的照片會非常有幫助,不想給的部分當然可以塗掉。

寫信之前:最常遇到的三件事

  • 某個欄位辨識錯了,或是空的。在確認畫面點一下該欄位,再點名片照片上的那段文字,App 就會把你點的 內容填進去。辨識完全在你的裝置上進行,不可能完美——反光的名片、特殊排版、或斜著拍的照片尤其容易出錯。
  • 我買了 Pro 但沒有生效。打開升級畫面,點還原購買。購買綁定你的 Apple 帳號,所以同一個帳號 登入的其他裝置也會生效。如果還是沒有出現,請來信,不要再買一次。
  • 我的名片存在哪裡?換手機怎麼搬?只存在你的裝置上,不在任何伺服器。到設定 → 備份匯出備份檔, 放到你自己掌控的地方(AirDrop、檔案 App、電腦),再到新手機還原。加密的 .piankevault 備份需要 你當初設定的密碼——密碼遺失就打不開了,我們也打不開,因為我們從來沒有那個密碼。

回報問題

最有用的回報是最小的、可以重現的那種:你做了什麼、你預期會發生什麼、實際發生了什麼。歡迎附截圖。 請不要寄出你不希望陌生人看到的內容——電子郵件並不私密,我們寧可收到一個模糊的回報,也不希望收到 你客戶的聯絡資料。

支援做不到的事

這支 App 沒有帳號系統,你的資料除了你的裝置以外不存在任何地方。那是整個設計的核心,而它的代價是真實的: 沒有備份就刪掉的名片是救不回來的,忘記的備份密碼也無法重設。這兩件事不是我們可以應要求為你解除的限制。

隱私權

iOS 版隱私權政策在 privacy-policy-ios.html。一句話版本: 這支 App 完全沒有網路程式碼,不收集任何資料。
